The arrangement's master level and limiter, after every track and effect.

| Control | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gain | 0% to 200% | 100% | The master level, after every generator and effect; 100% leaves the sound as it is. |
| Limit | 0 to 1 | 1 | A limiter on the whole mix that catches peaks before they clip; off leaves only the hard clip. |
| Ceiling | 50% to 100% | 95% | The level the limiter holds the mix under. |
