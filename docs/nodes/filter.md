# Filter

<!-- generated:begin — rhizome-docs rewrites this block; write outside it -->
A resonant filter after all the effects, taking away the highs, the lows or everything outside a band.

| Control | Range | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Filter | on or off | off | A resonant filter after all the effects, taking away the highs, the lows or everything outside a band. |
| Mix | 0 to 1 | 1 | How much filtered sound is blended with the unfiltered; full is all filtered. |
| Cutoff | 20 Hz to 20000 Hz | 2000 Hz | The frequency where the filter starts to cut. |
| Resonance | 0 to 1 | 0.2 | Emphasis around the cutoff: a gentle bump at low settings, a ringing whistle at high ones. |
| Type | 0 to 2 | 0 | Which part of the sound is kept: Low-pass lets the lows through, High-pass the highs, Band-pass a band around the cutoff. |
| Slope | 0 to 1 | 0 | How steeply the filter cuts past the cutoff: 12 dB is gentle, 24 dB is steep. |
| Tracking | 0 to 2 | 0 | How far the cutoff follows each step's pitch: off, half, or a full octave for every octave. |
